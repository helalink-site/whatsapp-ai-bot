50% before work starts, 30% after the demo, 20% on delivery. Payment by M-Pesa or bank transfer.

## How we work
1. Free consultation. 2. Written quote and agreement. 3. Build in stages. 4. Demo and feedback (two rounds of revisions included). 5. Launch and handover. 6. Optional monthly support.

## Included / not included
Included: the work in the written quote, two rounds of revisions, a demo and a handover session.
Not included (paid by the client directly to the provider): hosting and domain names; usage of third-party services such as AI, voice, SMS and M-Pesa fees; extra features beyond the agreed scope (quoted separately).
